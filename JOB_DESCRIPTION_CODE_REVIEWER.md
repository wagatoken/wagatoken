### 📋 **Position Overview**
We are seeking motivated **Blockchain Development Interns** with foundational knowledge in **Solidity**, **Next.js/React**, and **Web3 technologies** to participate in a comprehensive technical review of our production-ready WAGA Coffee Supply Chain MVP. ## 🎯 **Project Context**
**WAGA** is a revolutionary coffee supply chain platform that tokenizes coffee batches using **ERC-1155** standards, integrates **Zero-Knowledge proofs** for privacy, and leverages **Chainlink Functions** for real-world data verification. The platform enables **cooperatives**, **roasters**, **processors**, and **distributors** to create, manage, and trade tokenized coffee assets with full traceability.

### **Current Status:**
- ✅ **14 Smart Contracts** deployed and verified on **Base Sepolia**
- ✅ **Next.js Frontend** with three stakeholder portals
- ✅ **Role-based batch creation** system implemented
- ✅ **IPFS metadata storage** via Pinata
- ✅ **Zero-Knowledge privacy** layer integration
- ✅ **Comprehensive test suite** (58+ passing tests)
